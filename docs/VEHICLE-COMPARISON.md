## Speed/rpm consistency per gear — a smell, not a law

Within a gear, speed and rpm should scale together: `maxSpeed / minSpeed` ought to equal `maxEngineRPM / minEngineRPM`. Stretch a gear's speed window and leave the rpm band alone, and the record describes a gear whose ratio changes as you accelerate. It is **not** a hard rule — the Zonda Revo breaks it by 47 % and still ships — but it is the cheapest way to spot a gear that was edited by hand.
